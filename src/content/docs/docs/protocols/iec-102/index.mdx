IEC 60870-5-102 (IEC 102) is the companion standard of IEC 60870-5 for the **transmission of integrated totals in electric power systems**: reading the energy counters of electricity meters, period by period. A **controlling station** (a metering front end or data collector) reads an **integrated total station** (a meter or data concentrator) over a serial line, a modem or a terminal server. This page explains what matters for using the IEC 102 packages of [go-iec60870-5](/projects/go-iec60870-5/) and [iec102ctl](/projects/iec102ctl/).

## Link and frames

IEC 102 uses the FT 1.2 frames of IEC 60870-5-1 and the **unbalanced** link procedures of IEC 60870-5-2, like [IEC 101](/docs/protocols/iec-101/): the controlling station starts the link (request status of link, reset of remote link), sends a request with *send/confirm*, and polls class 2 data until the answer is complete. IEC 102 has **no spontaneous data**: a meter only answers what it is asked, so there is nothing to monitor between requests. Link address and common address are usually two octets.

## Records, totals and periods

IEC 102 has an application layer of its own, not the telecontrol ASDU of IEC 101 and 104. After the data unit identifier comes a **record address**, then the information objects; a list of integrated totals ends with one common time tag.

| Record address | Holds (as REE meters use them) |
|---|---|
| 11, 12 | Integration periods 1 and 2: the hourly (or quarter-hourly) load profile |
| 21 | Daily values |
| 1 | The accounting (billing) period |
| 50 to 55 | Lists of single-point events |

A request names a record, a range of **integrated total addresses** (in the REE profile: 1 active import, 2 active export, 3 to 6 the reactive quadrants) and a time window. The meter **confirms** the request (cause 7), sends one ASDU per integration period (cause 5) and **terminates** it (cause 10), or refuses it with a cause of refusal (13 to 18: no record, unknown type, unknown record number, unknown record address, no object, no period). Each total has a quality octet: overflow, adjusted, invalid.

## Time a and time b

IEC 102 has its own time formats, not the time tags of IEC 60870-5-4. **Time a** (minute to year, with tariff flags and the summer time bit SU) stamps the end of an integration period; **time b** adds seconds and milliseconds for the meter's clock. Meters keep local time, and the SU bit tells the repeated hour at the end of summer time apart.

## The Spanish REE profile

Spanish fiscal metering, run by Red Eléctrica (REE), uses IEC 102 with additions in the private range of type identifications: a **session** opened with an access key (C_AC_NA_2) and closed again (C_FS_NA_2), clock setting, tariff information of the contracts, contracted powers and special days, and other meanings for some quality bits. None of it is IEC 102 itself. go-iec60870-5 keeps it in a separate package and applies it only when asked.

<Aside type="note">
We do not hold the text of IEC 60870-5-102 or of the REE specification. The implementation is traced to vendor documentation of a commercial IEC 102 master, recorded meter traffic and independent implementations read for comparison, and the library classifies every type, cause and field as standard, REE profile, vendor-specific or unresolved.
</Aside>

## Where it is used

- Spanish fiscal metering of generation, transmission and large consumers
- Energy meters and data concentrators in substations and power plants
- Meter data collection for billing and settlement
